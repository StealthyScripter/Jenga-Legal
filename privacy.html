Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 15, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the Jenga Project Management mobile application ("Jenga PM").

1. Information We Collect

We collect account information (such as name and email), profile image from sign-in providers, and project data you create including milestones, budgets, expenses, diary entries, contacts, files, and other content necessary to operate the service.

2. How We Use Information

We use your data to authenticate users, provide core functionality, synchronize data across devices, improve app reliability and performance, and communicate important service-related updates.

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We may share data with trusted service providers required to operate the platform, authentication providers, or when required by law or legal process.

4. Third-Party Services

Jenga PM may use third-party services such as authentication providers, analytics tools, and cloud infrastructure providers. These services may process your data strictly on our behalf and in accordance with their own privacy policies.

5. Data Retention & Account Deletion

You may request deletion of your account and personal data at any time via the Account Deletion page (login required) or the Data Deletion Request form (no login required).

Upon requesting deletion, your account is immediately suspended and all active sessions are revoked. Your personal identifiers — name, email address, profile photo, and login credentials — will be permanently removed within 7 days.

Records retained for legal and compliance purposes: Certain operational records, including tasks performed, approvals granted, financial actions, inspection records, and audit logs, are retained even after your account is deleted. These records contain a snapshot of your display name captured at the time of the action. After deletion, your name in these records is replaced with "Deleted User" and the records can no longer be linked to your identity. Retention of these records is necessary for legal compliance, fraud prevention, audit traceability, and dispute resolution.

6. Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data, including encryption in transit and secure storage practices. However, no system is completely secure.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, export, or delete your personal data.

To delete your account (authenticated): visit jenga-pm.replit.app/account/delete. You will be asked to re-authenticate, review any project ownership that must be transferred, provide a reason, and confirm. Your account will enter a 7-day deletion window.

To request deletion without logging in: use our Data Deletion Request form. Requests are reviewed and processed within 72 hours.

As noted above, certain operational and audit records are retained after deletion for legal purposes. For any privacy-related questions, contact support@jengapm.app.

8. Children's Privacy

Jenga PM is not intended for children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised date.

10. Contact

For privacy-related inquiries, contact: support@jengapm.app
