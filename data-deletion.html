Data Deletion Request

Jenga PM — jengapm.replit.app

Use this form to request the deletion of your personal data from Jenga PM. You do not need to be logged in.

What gets deleted: your name, email address, profile photo, login credentials, and membership in all projects.

What is kept: project data, files, and history (transferred to other project members).

Deletion is processed within 72 hours of your request.
